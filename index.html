02 / A LITTLE EXPLORATION

Try these short examples. Open an explanation, follow the reasoning, and check what you notice.

01 — ALGEBRA

Finding the unknown.

2x + 5 = 17

Think of an equation as a balance. What you do to one side, do to the other.

Walk through it ＋
  1. Subtract 5 from both sides: 2x = 12.
  2. Divide both sides by 2: x = 6.
  3. Check: 2 × 6 + 5 = 17. The balance holds.
02 — GEOMETRY

Making space make sense.

A = ½ × b × h

A triangle takes up half the area of a parallelogram with the same base and height.

See an example ＋

For a base of 8 cm and perpendicular height of 5 cm:

  1. Multiply the base and height: 8 × 5 = 40.
  2. Take half: 40 ÷ 2 = 20 cm².
  3. Use square units because we measured area.
03 — NUMBER SENSE

Parts of a bigger picture.

⅓ + ¼ = ?

Before adding fractions, express them using pieces of the same size.

Put it together ＋
  1. A common denominator is 12.
  2. ⅓ = 4/12 and ¼ = 3/12.
  3. Add the numerators: (4 + 3)/12 = 7/12.
